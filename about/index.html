01 / Background

Background

I’m an investment professional based in Frankfurt, with experience across sovereign reserve management, fixed income, insurance portfolios and private markets.

Before moving to Germany, I worked in International Markets & Investments at the State Bank of Pakistan. Managing institutional portfolios through the pandemic recovery, the global inflation shock and the monetary-tightening cycle that followed made the relationship between market narratives, portfolio constraints and actual decisions difficult to ignore.

It also drew me towards more systematic investment analysis. I moved to Germany for Frankfurt School’s Master of Finance in Capital Markets and later worked on investment analytics and private-market portfolios at Allianz Investment Management.

I’m particularly interested in the point where markets, investment judgement and technology meet—and whether an idea remains useful when it encounters real data, changing regimes and imperfect information.
