01 / About

About me

Before moving to Germany, I worked at the State Bank of Pakistan in International Markets & Investments, managing institutional portfolios through the pandemic recovery, the global inflation shock and one of the sharpest monetary-tightening cycles in decades.

I later completed a Master of Finance in Capital Markets at Frankfurt School and worked at Allianz Investment Management on investment analytics and private-market portfolios.

What interests me most is whether an investment idea remains useful once it encounters real data, changing regimes and imperfect information.
